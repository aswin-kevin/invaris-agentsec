Attack an AI agent in your browser.

The real invaris-agentsec engine runs here with Python in WebAssembly: hidden instructions in documents, poisoned tool results, secret extraction and fake authority claims. No install, no account, nothing to deploy.

A small agent with tools. It may search and write drafts. It must never send email, reveal credentials or make payments, and the fake key sk-DEMO-… must never leave it. Every tool is simulated, so nothing is really sent or paid.
